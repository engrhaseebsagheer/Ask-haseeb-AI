# SwitchLess

SwitchLess is an Android digital-wellbeing app in development by Haseeb Sagheer. It reduces distraction with app usage limits, focus sessions and reminders that break repetitive app switching, and it tracks progress privately on the device.

Type: Product. Status: Building. Built by Haseeb Sagheer, solo.
What the status means: Building means the app is in development and has not been released yet. There is nothing to download today.
Portfolio page: https://haseebsagheer.com/projects/switchless/

## Overview
SwitchLess is an Android app for people who lose time to hopping between the same few apps. It is in development.

It works in three ways: limits on how long chosen apps can be used, focus sessions that hold distractions back for a set time, and reminders that appear when app switching turns into a loop.

Progress is tracked privately on the device. Optional Pro features will add advanced controls and insights.

## The problem SwitchLess solves
Most distraction is not one long session. It is switching between the same few apps over and over without deciding to.

## Who SwitchLess is for
People who lose time to switching between the same few apps without meaning to.

## What SwitchLess does
- Sets usage limits per app
- Runs focus sessions
- Reminds the user when they fall into repetitive app switching
- Tracks progress on the device, privately
- Offers optional Pro features for advanced controls and insights

## How SwitchLess works, step by step
1. Limit: Usage limits are set for chosen apps.
2. Focus: A focus session blocks out distractions.
3. Nudge: A reminder appears when switching becomes a loop.
