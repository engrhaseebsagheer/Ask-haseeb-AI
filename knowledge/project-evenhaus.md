# Evenhaus

Evenhaus is a shared chore app for roommates and couples, built by Haseeb Sagheer. It rotates chores automatically and gives each one effort points, so a balance screen shows who has done their fair share. It is free for the whole household.

Type: Product. Status: Early access. Built by Haseeb Sagheer, solo.
What the status means: Early access means the product is running and the website at evenhaus.me is taking early-access sign-ups. iPhone users can use the web version now; a native iOS app is planned.
Links: https://evenhaus.me
Portfolio page: https://haseebsagheer.com/projects/evenhaus/

## Overview
Evenhaus is one shared chore chart for a whole home. It rotates the chores, weighs each one by effort, and shows everyone's fair share, so nobody has to keep score in their head.

Most chore apps count tasks. Evenhaus counts effort. Each chore is worth points, so taking out the trash and cleaning the bathroom are not treated as equal. A balance view shows each person's points against their fair share for the week, and who is ahead or behind.

It is free for everyone in the home, with no ads. Premium is optional and one purchase covers the whole household. Data is stored in the EU.

## The problem Evenhaus solves
Splitting chores by count is unfair, because taking out the trash is not the same work as cleaning the bathroom. Evenhaus splits by effort.

## Who Evenhaus is for
Flat-shares, student houses and couples.

## What Evenhaus does
- Rotates the home’s chores automatically
- Gives each chore effort points (trash is 1 point, cleaning the bathroom is 3)
- Shows a balance screen for this week and this month
- Works on Android and on the web for iPhone and computer users
- Stores data in the EU (Frankfurt), with no ads and no tracking

## How Evenhaus works, step by step
1. List: The household lists its chores.
2. Rotate: Evenhaus assigns and rotates them.
3. Score: Each completed chore adds its effort points.
4. Balance: The balance screen shows who is ahead and who is behind.

## Engineering notes for Evenhaus
- Effort points: Each chore is worth points that reflect the effort. Taking out the trash is 1 point and cleaning the bathroom is 3.
- Automatic rotation: Chores rotate between people without anyone having to assign them.
- Balance screen: One screen shows who has done their fair share this week and this month.
- Privacy: Data is stored in the EU, in Frankfurt. There are no ads and no tracking.
