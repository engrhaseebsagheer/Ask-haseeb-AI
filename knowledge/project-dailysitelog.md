## Overview
DailySiteLog is daily report software for small construction contractors. The site lead talks through the day, adds photos, and sends the client a sealed PDF.

The idea behind it is that a daily log is insurance. When a client disputes a delay or an invoice, the daily record is the first thing anyone asks for, and it only counts if it is complete, dated and unchanged. That is hard to produce on paper at the end of a shift.

So the product removes the writing. Reports are spoken, photos carry a verified time and GPS stamp, the finished report is sealed, and it all works offline for sites with no signal. I designed, built and run it myself.

## The problem DailySiteLog solves
Construction daily reports are often written on paper or sent as chat messages, which makes them hard to find later when a delay or dispute needs evidence.

## Who DailySiteLog is for
Small contractors who need a dated, complete daily record without doing paperwork at the end of a shift.

## What DailySiteLog does
- Turns a spoken summary of the day into a written report
- Stamps photos with a verified time and GPS location
- Produces a sealed PDF report for the client
- Works offline on site
- Sends a weekly email to the client
- Is free for one project, with no card required

## How DailySiteLog works, step by step
1. Talk: The site lead talks through the day.
2. Photos: Photos are added with a verified time and GPS stamp.
3. Seal: The report is sealed as a PDF.
4. Send: The client receives the report.

## Engineering notes for DailySiteLog
- Voice to report: The site lead talks through the day and the app turns it into the written report.
- Evidence: Photos carry a verified time and GPS stamp, and the finished report is sealed as a PDF.
- Offline first: It works on site without a connection.
- Hosting: The database runs on Supabase. The site is hosted on Vercel and deploys automatically.
