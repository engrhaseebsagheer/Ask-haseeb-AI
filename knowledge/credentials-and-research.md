# Credentials and research of Haseeb Sagheer

## Published research
Title: Role of AI-Enhanced Education System in Empowering Students with Life Skills.
Published in the Research Journal for Social Affairs, August 2025. DOI 10.71317/RJSA.003.05.0373.
It is an empirical study of how AI-based education systems affect life skills in higher education: adaptability, problem-solving, collaboration and critical thinking.
- Quantitative study with 400 participants, using stratified random sampling
- Analysis with t-tests, ANOVA and regression
- Identified adaptive learning systems, simulations and AI tutors as the key tools for skill development
- Found AI adoption to be a significant predictor of life-skills outcomes
